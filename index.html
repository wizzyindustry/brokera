How BROKERA Works

Safe, broker-backed trading step by step.

1

Post or Discover

Sellers upload goods into the Available Pool while keeping contact details private. Buyers easily search verified listings.

2

Broker Assignment

A licensed, verified broker claims the listing exclusively, acting as the trusted point of contact for negotiations.

3

Transparent Offers

Buyers submit offers through the platform. The broker coordinates inspects, counter-offers, and deal terms.

4

Secure Handover

Payment clears safely, commission is calculated automatically, and the item changes hands smoothly.
