Our Mission & Model

Empowering Confident Sourcing & Trading in Tanzania

BROKERA is a web-based brokerage marketplace connecting Sellers, Verified Brokers, and Buyers. We solve the trust gap in local peer-to-peer commerce.

Tri-Party Marketplace

Designed for every participant in the deal.

For Sellers

Privacy & Peace of Mind

List your goods with photos, target price, and location. Your personal contact details remain private — brokers manage buyer inquiries for you.

For Brokers

Verified Commission Opportunities

Claim available listings in your area, connect with interested buyers, negotiate terms, and earn transparent, locked-in commissions.

For Buyers

Verified Sourcing & Selection

Browse genuine marketplace items, submit formal offers online, and let a verified local broker facilitate item inspection and deal closing.

End-to-End Workflow

From listing post to successful handover.

01

1. Post Listing

Seller uploads item specs, location, and photos. It enters the Available Goods Pool immediately.

02

2. Claim Listing

A verified broker claims exclusive management of the listing for a fixed duration window.

03

3. Buyer Offer

Buyer browses the pool and submits an offer. The broker reviews and coordinates counter-offers.

04

4. Closed Deal

Payment clears, commission calculates automatically, and the item is handed over securely.
